Rules made on a cut-off pod

When a pod is cut off from goat, a person named as its local authority can let a source in, shut one out, extend or bring back a rule, for that pod only and always with an end time. When the pod connects again it reports each change here. As org lead of the pod's org, you decide each one: Ratify keeps it as a goat rule; Let lapse ends it. Data sent under a waiting rule is marked "local rule, not yet ratified". A revoke made in goat always wins over a local rule.

Same as goat carriage pending, goat carriage ratify <id> and goat carriage lapse <id>.
