Log Analyzer

Paste your zenohd or publisher stderr on the left. The analyzer runs a curated catalog of error-signature regexes and surfaces structured findings with the exact step to take. Common failure shapes are caught in one paste — peer-vs-client mode, TLS trust gaps, ACL denies, version skew, the classic S3-vs-channel subscriber miss.

Nothing is persisted. The paste is parsed in memory and dropped. If a finding doesn't apply, the catalog table below lists every signature so you can see what we know about — helpful when our answer is "the analyzer found nothing."
