    If it is blocked: Details for your network team.

    For a person, this is the organization or unit your access is grouped under. For a system, this is the organization that operates this system. It becomes your organization of record: it groups you with your colleagues, it is what data-sharing decisions are made about, and your teammates should all type the same name here.

    Your own citizenship — not your organization's nation. The two are allowed to differ, and often do: a liaison officer, or anyone posted to a command of another country. We do not infer one from the other, and your organization's own nation is recorded separately by the administrator who registers it. Your citizenship can limit which shared data you may read — some shared data lanes your organization can reach are narrowed by the nationality of the person reading, which is why it is required: a request without it cannot be reviewed.
