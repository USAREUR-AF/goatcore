# Local contact routing

**Who can help me get started?** Ask any available GOAT onboarding or support team member. They can walk you through the steps or privately connect you with whoever can help. You do not need to find an individual first.

The monitored destination that a newcomer can reach before GOAT access is **Pending CODEOWNER confirmation**. This is a known gap until the path is confirmed and tested; do not publish a placeholder or personal contact.

These nine topics are examples, not separate front doors. A newcomer starts with **Ask for help** and the teammate who responds routes the question privately. A documented position appears only where the cited source assigns that responsibility. Routing does not grant approval authority, permission to release data, or authority to issue or rotate a certificate.

| Example topic | Where the newcomer starts | Responsible role or authority |
|---|---|---|
| General onboarding | **GOAT onboarding or support team (any available member)** | The responding teammate guides or routes the question |
| Workstation support | **Ask for help** | **Pending CODEOWNER confirmation** |
| Portal and service owner | **Ask for help** | **Pending CODEOWNER confirmation** |
| Environment administrator | **Ask for help** | **Goat administrator** — troubleshooting assigns re-onboarding and diagnostic follow-up to this position |
| Platform and CA operator | **Ask for help** | **Goat environment operator** — the federation runbook assigns the procedure and certificate exchange to each environment’s operator |
| Data-release authority | **Ask for help** | **Pending CODEOWNER confirmation** — the source defines the decision but does not name the responsible team or position |
| Incident-response contact | **Ask for help** | **Pending CODEOWNER confirmation** |
| Partner contact and delegation verifier | **Ask for help** | **Pending CODEOWNER confirmation** |
| Backup contact when the primary is absent | **Ask for help** | **Pending CODEOWNER confirmation** |

Role-name mapping used in the cards:

- `issuer`, `invitation issuer`, `onboarding contact`, `onboarding lead`, and `onboarding owner` start at **Ask for help**; the responding teammate routes the question privately.
- `operator`, `environment owner`, and `environment administrator` map to **Environment administrator** unless a card explicitly names the platform and CA operator.
- `portal operator` and `service owner` map to **Portal and service owner** pending CODEOWNER confirmation.
- `local release authority` maps to **Data-release authority**.
- Partner authority and delegation checks map to **Partner contact and delegation verifier**.
- `backup contact` and a primary contact’s absence map to **Backup contact when the primary is absent**.
