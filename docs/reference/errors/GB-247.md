# GB-247: the site or class named is not declared

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-247` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-247` or `goatbuilder explain GB-247`.

## What it means

standup and join-node act on a site goatbuilder.yaml declares under the environment, and join-node on a class that site declares and the verb converges (core, obs, breakglass, network-jump). The one named is not there.

## Why it happens

- a misspelt site or class
- the site or class is not declared yet

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
the names declared are listed in the message; pass one of them
declare the site under environments[].sites, or the class under its classes:, in ops/goatbuilder/goatbuilder.yaml
```
