# GB-412: an image step did not run, because what it stands on is not there

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-412` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-412` or `goatbuilder explain GB-412`.

## What it means

Each step of `goatbuilder image <model> --step <name>` runs only when the step before it is proven, and when it has what it needs: the card (`--device`) for flashing or reading it back, the machine (`--host`, as ssh reaches it) for a step that runs on it, and on the machine the builder's own copy of the step's script, byte for byte this checkout's (the card carries it from the flash). The message names what is missing, the step whose proof does not hold and how to read it, or the script that differs. Nothing ran.

## Why it happens

- --device or --host was left out
- the step before was not run, or its proof does not hold yet (a reboot not made, an unlock not done)
- the card was flashed from another checkout, or the script was edited on the Pi

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder image <model> --step <the step named> --check --host <user@address>
flash the card again from this checkout, or copy the script to /boot/firmware on the Pi
```
