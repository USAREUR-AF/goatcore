# GB-327: grow was given a config that is not a goatlite

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-327` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-327` or `goatbuilder explain GB-327`.

## What it means

Growing a goatlite turns its goatlite.yaml (preset: lite, with its lite: block) into a full goatbuilder.yaml. The config given does not declare preset: lite, or has no lite: block, so there is nothing to grow. Nothing was written.

## Why it happens

- the config is already a full one (preset: full, or no preset)
- the lite: block was removed by hand

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
grow the goatlite.yaml the goatlite was rendered from
```
