# GB-193: our verifying key cannot be read, or does not verify our half

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-193` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-193` or `goatbuilder explain GB-193`.

## What it means

Phase 5 shows our verifying key to read aloud, derived from the Ed25519 seed that signs our package (FED_SIGNING_KEY: 64 hex characters or 32 bytes base64). It is unset, unreadable or not a seed, or the key it gives does not verify the signature on our half, so the partner would bind a key that cannot verify our package.

## Why it happens

- FED_SIGNING_KEY is not set in this environment
- it names a file that is not the package-signing seed, or a different seed than phase 3 signed with

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
set FED_SIGNING_KEY to the seed that signed our package (the same one phase 3 uses)
```
