# GB-260: a rollback has no history to go back to, or its target is refused

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-260` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-260` or `goatbuilder explain GB-260`.

## What it means

rollback goes back through the host's deploy ledger and the in-repo history (deploy-history.yml). There is no history for the component, not enough of it, or the ref it would roll to is flagged do_not_roll_to, or a host runs a ref neither history knows.

## Why it happens

- the component has not been adopted into deploy-history.yml
- the history holds fewer entries than the rollback asks for
- the target ref is flagged do_not_roll_to in the in-repo ledger
- a host runs a ref no history records (deployed by hand?)

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder rollback <component> --status
investigate the host the message names before rolling back
```
