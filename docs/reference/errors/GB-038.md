# GB-038: --from preprod-rows cannot resume this candidate

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-038` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-038` or `goatbuilder explain GB-038`.

## What it means

resuming at the preprod rows needs preprod to run the candidate on main and exactly one open record for it; one of the two is missing.

## Why it happens

- the preprod pin was never landed for this candidate
- the record was closed, or a second one opened

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder release <component> --tag <tag> --from preprod --ack '<name>' (pins preprod and opens the record)
```
