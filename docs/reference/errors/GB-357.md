# GB-357: a member deviation no longer names the value its goatnet resolves to

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-357` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-357` or `goatbuilder explain GB-357`.

## What it means

A member host-group's deviation in ops/pins/fleet-pins.yaml carries `over:`, the value its goatnet resolved the component to when the deviation was written. The goatnet now resolves it to something else: a goatnet deviation was added or changed (a hotfix), or fleet moved (a graduation, a fastlane). The member deviation still wins by precedence, so that member would keep its old pin and silently miss the change. Nothing was rendered.

## Why it happens

- a hotfix or other goatnet deviation was added after the member deviation was written
- fleet moved, by graduation or fastlane, and the member deviation was not looked at

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
delete the member deviation, so the member takes the goatnet's value (the usual answer for a hotfix)
or, to keep the member's pin deliberately, set the `over:` line the message prints under that member deviation
```
