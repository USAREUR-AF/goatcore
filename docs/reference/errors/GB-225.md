# GB-225: resources of the peering are still present after the back-out

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-225` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-225` or `goatbuilder explain GB-225`.

## What it means

The survey run after the removal still finds resources of this peering, or the removal play stopped partway. Every step is idempotent: running the back-out again removes what is left and reports the rest as absent.

## Why it happens

- a host became unreachable during the removal
- a unit or container would not stop

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder federate backout <partner>
```
