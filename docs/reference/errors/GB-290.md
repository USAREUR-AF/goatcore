# GB-290: the staged IPsec certificate is not right for this link

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-290` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-290` or `goatbuilder explain GB-290`.

## What it means

Before an IPsec credential rotation moves anything, goat-peering-edge checks the staged certificate: its key is the staged key, it chains to the CA it must chain to (our current CA for ipsec-leaf, the staged new CA for ipsec-ca), its subject CN is peering_edge_local_id, its key is ECDSA P-384 (secp384r1) as the proposals require, it is valid now, and it stays valid for peering_edge_min_valid_days more days (default 30). The message names each check that failed. Nothing was moved.

## Why it happens

- the leaf was issued for another peering or another identity
- the leaf was issued by another CA, or the staged key belongs to another certificate
- the leaf uses another key type, or expires too soon

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
have the ceremony issue the leaf again with Subject CN = peering_edge_local_id and an ECDSA P-384 key, from the right CA, then stage it and run the rotation again
```
