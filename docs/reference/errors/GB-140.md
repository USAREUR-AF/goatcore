# GB-140: our CA certificate is missing or is not a CA

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-140` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-140` or `goatbuilder explain GB-140`.

## What it means

the certificate phase 8 signs the peering leaves with does not exist, is not a PEM certificate, or is not a CA (basicConstraints CA:TRUE).

## Why it happens

- FED_CA_CERT, goat_federation_ca_cert or the derived ops/ansible/enrollment/ca/&lt;site>/root-cert.pem names a missing file
- a leaf certificate was named instead of the CA

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
set goat_federation_ca_cert in group_vars/<edge group>/ to our own CA certificate (never the partner's)
```
