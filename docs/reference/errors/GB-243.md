# GB-243: a standup names no site

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-243` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-243` or `goatbuilder explain GB-243`.

## What it means

standup stands up one site and needs --site. It may be left out only on a goatlite run, where goatlite.yaml (preset lite) names the one site the environment has.

## Why it happens

- --site was left out on an environment that is not a goatlite

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder standup <environment> --site <site>
```
