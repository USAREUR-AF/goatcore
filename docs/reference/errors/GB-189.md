# GB-189: a stage of `goatbuilder federate` was asked for the wrong way

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-189` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-189` or `goatbuilder explain GB-189`.

## What it means

The stages `status`, `deploy` and `test` take the partner after them and run their own phases; `--plan` and `--approve-plan` belong to `deploy`, whose start is phase 10 or, once the admission is done by hand, 11. Nothing ran.

## Why it happens

- the partner was left out, or given after a peering name instead of a stage word
- --to, --resume or a --from other than 10 or 11 was given to a stage
- --plan or --approve-plan was given outside `deploy`
- `rotate <partner> <key>` named a key whose rotation is designed and not built in this goatbuilder (`transport` or `signing`; this goatbuilder rotates the leaves and the carrier keys), or a key it does not know; the message says what is missing (for `transport`, that the partner cannot yet receive our new package)

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder federate deploy <partner> --approve-plan <hash> (or --from 11 after the admission)
goatbuilder federate test <partner>
goatbuilder federate rotate <partner> leaves (or carrier: the keys this goatbuilder rotates; the message lists them)
```
