## What it means

Phase 5 or 6 was asked to confirm (by --confirm or a value) and something is not there yet: their half or CA certificate has not arrived, a value read aloud was not given and there is no terminal to ask at, or the person did not type yes. The run stopped there, as a by-hand stop does (exit 0); a stop at phase 5 writes no keyring.

## Why it happens

- the partner has not published yet
- one of --partner-key, --partner-ca-fingerprint, --agreement-digest is missing
