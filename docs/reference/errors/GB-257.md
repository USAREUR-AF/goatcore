# GB-257: fleet-pins.yaml is not in the shape goatbuilder edits

## What it means

goatbuilder reads fleet-pins.yaml's components and edits a component's block in place. The file has no components:, or the component's block or its fleet: line is not where the writer looks.

## Why it happens

- fleet-pins.yaml has no components: mapping
- the component's block was edited by hand into another shape

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder pins --check (the table as goatbuilder reads it)
restore the component's block to `<component>:` with a `fleet:` line, as the others are
```
