# GB-411: goatbuilder image cannot build this model, or the builder has no such step

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-411` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-411` or `goatbuilder explain GB-411`.

## What it means

`goatbuilder image <model>` runs a model's golden path when that model's builder is one goatbuilder wraps: today `ops/rpi-base-image/` for `rpi5`, read from the model's entry in its family's file under ops/goatbuilder/families/: its `builder` and its `os.golden_image` (file, sha256, url). The model is not there, its builder is another (a Karbon's or a Jetson's script, which goatbuilder does not run yet), its golden image entry is missing or malformed, or `--step` names a step the builder does not have. Nothing ran.

## Why it happens

- the model's name is misspelt, or it is a model of another builder
- the model's golden_image entry was removed or changed by hand
- --step names a step of another unlock or console (yubikey is not a step with --unlock passphrase; headless-unlock only with --headless)

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder image rpi5 --dry-run (lists the steps)
build another model by its own builder's steps, then check it: goatbuilder doctor --target <type> --site <site> --live
```
