# GB-288: a health publisher a joining site declares has no play in the checkout

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-288` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-288` or `goatbuilder explain GB-288`.

## What it means

join-node deploys each health publisher the end-state model marks present at a joining site (endstate.py model &lt;site>, from the pin table) after the site's data router, by running the publisher's play limited to the node. The model marks a publisher present whose play (ops/ansible/&lt;publisher>-deploy.yml) is not in this checkout, so the plan was refused before any phase ran.

## Why it happens

- the checkout is an older or partial tree that does not carry the publisher's play
- the pin table declares a publisher at the site that the site does not mean to run

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
run join-node from a checkout that carries ops/ansible/<publisher>-deploy.yml
declare the publisher absent at the site in ops/pins/fleet-pins.yaml, with its reason: components: <publisher>: deviations: <site>_hosts: {absent: true}
goatbuilder endstate <env> shows what the model marks present at each site
```
