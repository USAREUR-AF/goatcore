# GB-030: no host of the group is in the loaded inventories

## What it means

the group is declared in the pins but the inventories loaded for this goatnet carry no host for it, so nothing was deployed (declared, not built).

## Why it happens

- a site declared ahead of its hardware
- the wrong inventory pair for this goatnet
