# GB-186: the plan was not approved

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-186` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-186` or `goatbuilder explain GB-186`.

## What it means

`goatbuilder federate deploy` executes a plan only when approval names that plan's hash: `--approve-plan <hash>`, or a person at a terminal typing yes after reading the plan and its hash. `--yes` never approves a plan. No approval was given, or it named another hash, so nothing changed.

## Why it happens

- there is no terminal and --approve-plan was not passed
- --approve-plan names the hash of an older plan (the plan was made again since)
- the person answered anything but yes

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
read the plan, then: goatbuilder federate deploy <partner> --approve-plan <the hash it prints>
goatbuilder federate plan <partner> (a new plan, when the old one is out of date)
```
