# GB-360: a machine's record was not read

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-360` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-360` or `goatbuilder explain GB-360`.

## What it means

The converger asked one machine for its newest record of a component, and the machine was not read: it did not answer, the inventory names it and it was silent, or its read FAILED. Contract C7: such a machine is unknown, never done, and nothing about its record is assumed.

## Why it happens

- the host is down, or not reachable from this machine over the transport the environment uses
- the inventory names a host that gave no answer
- the read ran on the host and FAILED (become refused, no python)

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder status --env <environment> (each machine's state, and why it was not read)
goatbuilder doctor --env <environment> (the transport and each host's reach)
```
