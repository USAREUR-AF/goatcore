# GB-256: a deploy play's hosts cannot be resolved

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-256` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-256` or `goatbuilder explain GB-256`.

## What it means

To target a deploy, goatbuilder reads the component's play for its hosts: line, following at most three import_playbook hops. The play has neither, or the chain is longer.

## Why it happens

- the play has no hosts: line and no import_playbook
- more than three import_playbook hops

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
give the play a hosts: line ("{{ target }}" for a play targeted per group)
declare deploy_playbook in fleet-pins.yaml for the play that does
```
