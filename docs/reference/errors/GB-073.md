# GB-073: the goatbuilder download surface could not be read

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-073` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-073` or `goatbuilder explain GB-073`.

## What it means

update reads version.json, the platform binary and its .sha256 from get.&lt;site>.&lt;zone>/goatbuilder/. One of them did not arrive: the connection failed, the server answered with an error, the answer was not what the surface publishes, or the binary stopped arriving for 30 seconds. Nothing was installed; the binary in place is the one that ran.

## Why it happens

- this machine is not on the goatnet's mesh (get.&lt;site> is mesh-only)
- the surface is mid-deploy, or does not publish this platform's binary
- the link stalled; a slow link is fine, a silent one for 30 seconds is not

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
curl -fsS https://get.<site>.<zone>/goatbuilder/version.json (is the surface reachable from here?)
retry the update; it is idempotent
GOATBUILDER_UPDATE_STALL_SECS=120 goatbuilder update (a slower link)
```
