# GB-005: no deploy playbook resolves for the component

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-005` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-005` or `goatbuilder explain GB-005`.

## What it means

neither &lt;ledger>-deploy.yml nor &lt;component>-deploy.yml exists in ops/ansible, or the declared deploy_playbook is not there.

## Why it happens

- the component is deployed by another play and does not declare it
- the playbook was renamed

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
declare deploy_playbook: <play>.yml on the component in ops/pins/fleet-pins.yaml
goatbuilder doctor --all (the playbook row names what was looked for)
```
