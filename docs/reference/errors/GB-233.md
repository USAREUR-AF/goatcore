# GB-233: a peering's federation.toml cannot be generated from the environment's

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-233` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-233` or `goatbuilder explain GB-233`.

## What it means

The peering declares federation_toml: rendered, so goatbuilder federate allocate generates its federation.toml from the environment's own ops/federation/&lt;environment>/federation.toml, the one owner of sovereign_id, term_days, cut_contact, the release sets, the accept limits and the measured path MTU, and fills [transport] from the allocated values. That file is missing, is not TOML, or lacks a required top-level value. Nothing was written.

## Why it happens

- the environment has no ops/federation/&lt;environment>/federation.toml yet
- sovereign_id, term_days or cut_contact is missing from it

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
write ops/federation/<environment>/federation.toml once for the environment (the template goat federate init prints is the shape)
or declare federation_toml: hand on the peering and keep writing its federation.toml by hand
```
