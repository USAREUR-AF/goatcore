# GB-289: the IPsec rotation's staged material is missing or unreadable

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-289` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-289` or `goatbuilder explain GB-289`.

## What it means

A credential rotation of an ipsec-fips peering (goat-peering-edge with peering_edge_rotation set) switches the edge to a new leaf that the offline ceremony issued and an operator staged beside the live files: &lt;name>-cert.next.pem in the swanctl x509 directory, &lt;name>-key.next.pem in its private directory at mode 0600, and for an ipsec-ca rotation our new CA as &lt;name>-local-ca.next.pem in x509ca. The rotation also needs our current IPsec CA certificate at peering_edge_local_ca_path to check the new leaf's chain. One of them is missing, unreadable, or the key is not mode 0600. The role refused before anything moved.

## Why it happens

- the new leaf, its key, or (for ipsec-ca) our new CA was not staged on this edge
- peering_edge_local_ca_path is not set, or names no file on the edge
- the staged key was copied without mode 0600

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
stage what the ceremony issued at the paths the message names, with the key at mode 0600, then run the rotation again
set peering_edge_local_ca_path in the edge's group_vars to our current per-peering IPsec CA certificate
```
