# GB-273: the partner's acceptance result is not from this session

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-273` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-273` or `goatbuilder explain GB-273`.

## What it means

The partner's signed acceptance result is for another agreement, session or attempt, is signed by or for another party, or does not echo the challenge issued to us, so it cannot be from this session's samples. It is refused before its signature is checked, and nothing from it is combined (docs/design/federation-acceptance.md §6.1).

## Why it happens

- an earlier session's or attempt's result was read again (a replay, or a result left from before)
- the two sides hold different agreements: the digests read aloud at phase 6 differ
- our own result was read back as if it were the partner's

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
compare the agreement digest and the session id with the partner on the call
run phase 14 again on both sides in one session
```
