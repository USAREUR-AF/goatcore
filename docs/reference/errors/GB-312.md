# GB-312: a carrier rotation was asked for on a peering whose forwarder is declared by hand

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-312` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-312` or `goatbuilder explain GB-312`.

## What it means

`goatbuilder federate rotate <partner> carrier` makes the edge's new carrier key in phase 2, on the edge. The forwarder must then hold that key: its entry for the peering, peering_forwarder_peerings[name=&lt;partner>].edge_carrier_public_key, applied by peering-forwarder.yml. This peering's forwarder is declared by hand (peering_wg_via_forwarder: true in its edge group's group_vars; goatbuilder.yaml does not declare it), so that entry is in the operator's own group_vars, which the rotation cannot change, and its new value cannot be known before the rotation runs. The rotation would leave the carrier down, so it stopped before anything ran: no key was set aside and no rotation id was kept.

## Why it happens

- the deployment declares its peering forwarder in hand-written group_vars rather than with forwarder: on the peering in goatbuilder.yaml

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
declare the forwarder on the peering in ops/goatbuilder/goatbuilder.yaml (forwarder: with its endpoint), then: goatbuilder federate allocate (review and commit what it writes)
goatbuilder federate rotate <partner> carrier (phase 2 then runs peering-forwarder.yml with the edge's new key)
```
