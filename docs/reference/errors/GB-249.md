# GB-249: the fixture's standup does not fit what was asked

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-249` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-249` or `goatbuilder explain GB-249`.

## What it means

standup --mock rehearses the standup a fixture's converger.yaml declares for one environment and site, against that fixture's goatbuilder.yaml. The environment or site asked for is another, or a phase of the rehearsal names a site or class the declaration does not allow (a joiner may touch only the founder it attaches to; a class needs machines).

## Why it happens

- --mock names a fixture for another environment or site
- the fixture's converger.yaml and goatbuilder.yaml disagree

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder standup <the fixture's environment> --site <its site> --mock <fixture>
correct the fixture's converger.yaml standup: or its goatbuilder.yaml
```
