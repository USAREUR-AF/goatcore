# GB-364: a health publisher join-node deploys has no leaf in the site's leaf manifest

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-364` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-364` or `goatbuilder explain GB-364`.

## What it means

join-node deploys each health publisher the end-state model marks present at a joining site, and each one runs with its own leaf certificate, which phase 13 mints from the site's leaf manifest (ops/enrollment/manifests/&lt;site>-certs.yaml) and stages onto the node. The model marks a publisher present whose leaf the manifest does not carry, so the plan was refused before any phase ran, rather than at staging or at deploy.

## Why it happens

- the manifest was written before the publisher was declared at the site
- the pin table declares a publisher at the site that the site does not mean to run

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
add a leaf named for the publisher to ops/enrollment/manifests/<site>-certs.yaml, as ops/enrollment/manifests/TEMPLATE-certs.yaml shows
declare the publisher absent at the site in ops/pins/fleet-pins.yaml, with its reason: components: <publisher>: deviations: <site>_hosts: {absent: true}
goatbuilder endstate <env> shows what the model marks present at each site
```
