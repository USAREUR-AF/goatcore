# GB-143: the peering inventory does not reach the edge or core

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-143` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-143` or `goatbuilder explain GB-143`.

## What it means

inventory/peering-edges.ini is missing, has no group for this peering's edge or core, lists no host in it, or does not list the group under [peering_edges:children] / [peering_cores:children]. A play limited to such a group runs against nothing and reports success.

## Why it happens

- the edge or core group was never added to the deployment's inventory
- the committed inventory ships the host commented out

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
add [<partner>_edge] and [<partner>_core] with their hosts, and list each group under its parent's :children
```
