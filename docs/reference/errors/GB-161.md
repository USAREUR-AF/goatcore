# GB-161: a peering's partner id cannot name a forwarder entry

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-161` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-161` or `goatbuilder explain GB-161`.

## What it means

The forwarder names each peering it relays by the partner id, and its role accepts 2-16 characters of a-z 0-9 and hyphen. This partner id is outside that, so the forwarder would refuse the entry.

## Why it happens

- the peering's with: holds an underscore, or is one character or longer than 16

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
declare the partner by its sovereign id (lower-case letters, digits, hyphens)
```
