# GB-387: a Spock slot is lost and can never be read again

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-387` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-387` or `goatbuilder explain GB-387`.

## What it means

`goatbuilder join-node ... --slots` (the detector, `spock-reseed.py slots`) read every Spock subscription and slot of the node, in every database with a Spock node, and at least one slot reads `wal_status = lost` (or `conflicting`): Postgres removed WAL the slot still needed, after it held more than `max_slot_wal_keep_size`. The subscription it feeds cannot catch up, whatever it reports, and the rows it missed reach the other side only by a rebuild. A slot at a provider is the node's inbound side; a slot on the node is a subscriber's outbound side. The detector changed nothing.

## Why it happens

- the node, or the site holding the slot, was cut off long enough for the other side to write more than its slot cap
- a subscription was disabled for a long time while its provider kept writing

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
rebuild the node from its founder: goatbuilder join-node <env> --site <site> --node <node> --class <class> --reseed --dry-run
a lost slot on the node for a peer other than its founder is rebuilt with that peer: run the detector and the rebuild for that peer's site
read it by hand where the slot lives: SELECT slot_name, active, wal_status FROM pg_replication_slots;
```
