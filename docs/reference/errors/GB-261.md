# GB-261: the release's signature does not verify, so the update refuses

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-261` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-261` or `goatbuilder explain GB-261`.

## What it means

`goatbuilder update` verifies the keyless signature over the release's SHA256SUMS (SHA256SUMS.cosign-bundle) itself, offline, against the Sigstore trusted root: the certificate chains to a Fulcio CA, Rekor's signed entry timestamp verifies, the entry names this file, and the certificate is the release signer's goatbuilder-release.yml at the served version's goatbuilder/v&lt;version> tag. One of these failed, or the surface serves no signature. The message names the check. Nothing was installed.

## Why it happens

- the surface serves a SHA256SUMS or a binary that the release workflow did not sign
- the signature is another repository's, another workflow's, or another version's
- Sigstore rotated its Fulcio CA or Rekor key after this goatbuilder was built
- the surface serves no SHA256SUMS.cosign-bundle

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
check that the surface serves the release unchanged: the goat-get role's goat_get_goatbuilder_release_tag, and the release page's SHA256SUMS
after a Sigstore key rotation: GOATBUILDER_UPDATE_TRUSTED_ROOT=<a trusted_root.json holding the new key> goatbuilder update
goatbuilder -C <checkout> update, so that repo.release_source names the repository that publishes goatbuilder
```
