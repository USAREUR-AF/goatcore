# GB-079: federation declarations cannot be regenerated safely

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-079` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-079` or `goatbuilder explain GB-079`.

## What it means

gen-config.py stopped before replacing goatbuilder.yaml because a federation or posture source is missing, malformed, conflicts with fields owned by another source, uses a YAML value that cannot be compared deterministically, or omits declaration content present in the generated file. This includes a config written by goatbuilder grow until its sites, other operator-owned environment fields and peerings are copied into federation.yml. The output is unchanged.

## Why it happens

- an overlay that owns ops/goatbuilder/federation.yml is absent from the assembled checkout
- existing environment or peering declarations have not yet been migrated from goatbuilder.yaml into federation.yml
- a config written by goatbuilder grow has no declaration digest and its sites or peerings exist only in goatbuilder.yaml
- existing secrets.federation key or CA paths have not yet been migrated from goatbuilder.yaml into posture.yml
- federation.yml declares name, converger or groups, which remain owned by fleet-pins.yaml and ladder.yaml
- a source or generated output is not UTF-8, or the source contains duplicate keys or peering identities, non-text mapping keys, an unquoted date or another unsupported YAML scalar

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
restore the overlay's ops/goatbuilder/federation.yml, then run: python3 ops/goatbuilder/gen-config.py
move every operator-owned environment field and peering the error names into federation.yml, then regenerate once to record adoption
for a config written by goatbuilder grow, copy every site and other operator-owned environment field under the matching environment key in federation.yml, copy every peering unchanged into its peerings: list, then regenerate once to record adoption
move secrets.federation signing_key, ca_cert and ca_key paths into posture.yml, then regenerate once to record adoption
for intentional removal after adoption, keep federation.yml with explicit environments: {} and peerings: []
to remove adopted federation key and CA paths, keep posture.yml with store: and remove its federation: block
quote dates and mapping keys as text, remove duplicate keys, and follow the exact path in the error
```
