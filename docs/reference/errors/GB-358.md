# GB-358: a member deviation does not name the value it overrides, or `over:` is where it means nothing

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-358` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-358` or `goatbuilder explain GB-358`.

## What it means

Every member host-group deviation with a `value:` in ops/pins/fleet-pins.yaml names, in `over:`, what its goatnet resolves the component to without it: a ref, `absent`, or `none` (fleet null and no goatnet deviation). This one has no `over:`, or `over:` sits on a goatnet deviation or an `absent: true` one, where it means nothing. The message prints the line to paste. Nothing was rendered.

## Why it happens

- the table predates ADR 1256, so its member deviations carry no `over:`
- `over:` was added to a goatnet-scope or an absent member deviation

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
to keep the member's pin, paste the `over:` line the message prints under that member deviation
or delete the member deviation, so the member takes its goatnet's value
on a goatnet or an absent member deviation, delete the `over:` line
```
