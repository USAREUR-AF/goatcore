# GB-383: the goatnet's signed intent does not verify

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-383` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-383` or `goatbuilder explain GB-383`.

## What it means

Before any host, deploy (goatbuilder and deploy-component.sh) verifies the goatnet's signed intent from the checkout alone, with the checks `goatbuilder pins --check` makes of it: ops/consistency/intent/&lt;goatnet>/intent.json is there, its content_hash is its body's, its detached signature verifies against the committed public key of the CA it names, it is what the tree renders, and each member group's pins.yml is what the pins table renders. One does not hold, or could not be judged (openssl missing, a file that does not parse): the run is refused, and the line says which. A dry run reports it, prints the whole plan and exits 1. --verify-only deploys nothing and is not judged.

## Why it happens

- a pin was changed and the intent was not regenerated and signed again
- pins.yml or intent.json was edited by hand
- the goatnet's intent was never signed, or its CA's public key is not in ops/enrollment/public-keys/
- openssl, or for deploy-component.sh the Python module jsonschema, is not on this machine

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder pins --check (says what differs)
goatbuilder pins, then sign the intent with ops/enrollment/cmd/intent-sign and commit intent.json and intent.json.sig together
```
