# GB-074: the downloaded goatbuilder is not vouched for by its release

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-074` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-074` or `goatbuilder explain GB-074`.

## What it means

Before touching anything, update checks that the &lt;asset>.sha256 the surface serves agrees with the release's SHA256SUMS, that the SHA256SUMS signature verifies against the release workflow's identity (when cosign is on PATH), and that the downloaded binary hashes to that digest. One of these failed, so it refused to install; the installed binary is unchanged.

## Why it happens

- the surface is mid-deploy and the binary, its checksum and SHA256SUMS are from different releases
- the download was altered or truncated on the way
- the signature over SHA256SUMS is not from the release workflow of the repository goatbuilder.yaml names

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
retry in a few minutes; the surface's files are written together
goatbuilder update --check (what the surface serves now)
cosign verify-blob --bundle SHA256SUMS.cosign-bundle --certificate-identity-regexp '^https://github.com/<owner>/<repo>/' --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com SHA256SUMS (the check by hand)
```
