# GB-282: rotating our CA needs a decision before it can be built

## What it means

`goatbuilder federate rotate <partner> ca` would replace the CA that signs our peering leaves. In package v1 every check accepts exactly one CA per party, the one fingerprint our package carries (federation-package-spec.md §5.1), so the partner could not trust the new CA beside the old one while our leaves change over, and data would stop. The overlap of both CAs is decided (ADR 1242: our next CA is announced in package v2) and designed in the key-rotation design §4, but this goatbuilder does not build it yet. Nothing ran.

## Why it happens

- `rotate <partner> ca` was asked for, and this goatbuilder does not yet build the CA overlap ADR 1242 decides

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
rotate the leaves instead, under the same CA: goatbuilder federate rotate <partner> leaves
read ADR 1242 and the key-rotation design §4 for the CA overlap a later goatbuilder builds
```
