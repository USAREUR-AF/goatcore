# GB-282: rotating our CA needs a decision before it can be built

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-282` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-282` or `goatbuilder explain GB-282`.

## What it means

`goatbuilder federate rotate <partner> ca` would replace the CA that signs our peering leaves. Every check accepts exactly one CA per party, the one fingerprint our package carries (federation-package-spec.md §5.1), so the partner could not trust the new CA beside the old one while our leaves change over, and data would stop. The overlap of both CAs is designed in the key-rotation design §4 and needs an ADR superseding §5.1 before it is built. Nothing ran.

## Why it happens

- `rotate <partner> ca` was asked for, and no ADR yet lets a package carry a second CA fingerprint

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
rotate the leaves instead, under the same CA: goatbuilder federate rotate <partner> leaves
read the key-rotation design §4 for the overlap the ADR has to allow
```
