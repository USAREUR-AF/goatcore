# GB-346: the founder has not taken the node's own writes

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-346` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-346` or `goatbuilder explain GB-346`.

## What it means

Before the re-seed truncates the node's tables, the founder must hold everything the node wrote while it was away. The founder's subscription from the node does not read `replicating`, or its slot on the node has not confirmed the node's current WAL position within the wait (`--wait`, 600 seconds by default). Truncating now would lose those writes, so nothing was changed. When the founder's slot on the node is lost, the step passes instead: the founder can never take those writes, and the export step carries them.

## Why it happens

- the founder's subscription from the node is down or disabled
- the node's writes are still on their way: a large backlog, or a slow link
- the founder has no subscription from the node

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
on the founder: SELECT * FROM spock.sub_show_status(); the subscription from the node must read replicating
run the re-seed again; it starts at this step
```
