# GB-023: rollback refused or guided by artifact type

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-023` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-023` or `goatbuilder explain GB-023`.

## What it means

the component's artifact type is data, cert, forward-only or reversible:false (refused, exit 3), or config/acl (guided, exit 4): its rollback is not a pin flip.

## Why it happens

- the component carries a migration or a certificate

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder rollback <component> --status
follow the guided steps the row prints (config/acl)
```
