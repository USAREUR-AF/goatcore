# GB-034: the generated pins or intents differ from fleet-pins.yaml

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-034` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-034` or `goatbuilder explain GB-034`.

## What it means

`goatbuilder pins --check` regenerated every member's pins.yml and every goatnet's intent from ops/pins/fleet-pins.yaml and they differ from what is committed, or an intent is unsigned or tampered, or a governed pin is defined outside pins.yml.

## Why it happens

- fleet-pins.yaml changed without regenerating
- a hand edit to a generated pins.yml or intent

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder pins (regenerate), then re-sign the intents it names, then commit
```
