# GB-191: a value for confirmation 1 does not match

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-191` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-191` or `goatbuilder explain GB-191`.

## What it means

At phase 5 or 6 a value given by flag or typed is not the one the files hold: their CA fingerprint is not their package's, their CA certificate does not carry that fingerprint, their verifying key is not 32 bytes of base64 or does not verify their half, a sovereign id is not a plain id or is not the declared counterparty, or the agreement digest differs. The run stopped; at phase 5 no keyring was written, and at phase 6 the composed agreement is left for inspection.

## Why it happens

- a value was misheard or mistyped on the call
- the partner published a new package or CA after reading theirs aloud
- the two sides composed different agreements (their halves differ)

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
read the values back group by group with the partner, then run the same command again
goat federate diff <ours> <theirs> (names the field where two agreements differ)
```
