# GB-396: goat-xf-pipe-zenoh's conversion is not supplied

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-396` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-396` or `goatbuilder explain GB-396`.

## What it means

ops/ansible/goat-xf-pipe-deploy.yml installs goat-xf-pipe-zenoh (a release component, gate J24) and runs it as a systemd service, and what it converts is the goatnet's: goatcore holds no default. A member group that runs it sets goat_xf_pipe with from_format and to_format (pack names), in, out and metrics (a loopback or mesh &lt;address>:&lt;port> of its Prometheus listener), and zenoh_config (JSON5 text) when its Zenoh URIs name ?config=. One is missing, empty, not text, holds a control character, a format is not a pack name, or metrics is not such an address, so the play stopped at its first task. Nothing was installed.

## Why it happens

- the member group's group_vars do not set goat_xf_pipe
- one of from_format, to_format, in, out or metrics is missing or empty
- metrics is not a loopback (127.x, [::1]) or mesh (RFC 6598 shared address space) &lt;address>:&lt;port>
- a value holds a control character, a format is not a pack name, or zenoh_config is empty

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
write goat_xf_pipe in ops/ansible/group_vars/<member>/, as the play's header shows
./goat-xf-pipe-zenoh --help, in the release's archive, lists the formats and URIs it takes
```
