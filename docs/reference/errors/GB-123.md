# GB-123: a peering's pin is not usable

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-123` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-123` or `goatbuilder explain GB-123`.

## What it means

A pin under a peering's values: is malformed (an interface name over 15 characters, a subnet that is not a /31 or /30 on its boundary, a relative certificate directory, a name with spaces), or pins a forwarder value on a peering no forwarder relays.

## Why it happens

- a typing mistake in a pin
- public_port or a carrier_\* value pinned without forwarder:

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
correct the pin, or remove it to have the value allocated
declare forwarder: endpoint: on the peering, or remove the forwarder pins
```
