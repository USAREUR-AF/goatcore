# GB-187: the plan is not the one approved

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-187` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-187` or `goatbuilder explain GB-187`.

## What it means

The plan deploy was given is for another peering, or its hash changed between approval and execution (it is read again under the peering's lock, just before anything changes). Nothing changed. A plan that does not match its own hash is GB-201.

## Why it happens

- no plan has been made yet (&lt;work>/plan.json is missing)
- the plan was made again, or edited, after it was approved
- --plan names another peering's plan

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder federate plan <partner> (then read it and approve its hash)
```
