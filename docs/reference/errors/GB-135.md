# GB-135: the slot registry could not be read

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-135` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-135` or `goatbuilder explain GB-135`.

## What it means

sync-slot-anchors.py exited 2: the registry was unreadable, or it reported zero live slots. That is not a verdict on drift, and the check is not passed.

## Why it happens

- FED_SLOT_DB names a file that is not the portal registry, or it cannot be opened read-only
- the management host named by FED_SLOT_PG_SSH is unreachable, or its portal database container differs

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
check FED_SLOT_DB, or FED_SLOT_REGISTRY_STORE=postgres with FED_SLOT_PG_SSH (portal_store in group_vars/<goatnet>_hosts says which)
```
