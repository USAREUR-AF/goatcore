## What it means

the rows for a rung this laptop is not meshed to run goat on that rung's management host through its door (goat_via door:&lt;env>), and the door did not open or printed no ssh target.

## Why it happens

- aws-vault cannot reach the declared AWS profile (signed out, a malformed credential, or an expiring session)
- session-manager-plugin is not installed

## What to do
