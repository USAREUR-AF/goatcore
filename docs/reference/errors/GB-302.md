# GB-302: the IPsec link did not come up on the new leaf; the old pair was put back

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-302` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-302` or `goatbuilder explain GB-302`.

## What it means

After switching to the staged leaf, goat-peering-edge loaded the credentials, re-authenticated the IKE SA and waited up to peering_edge_rotation_confirm_seconds (the DPD timeout by default) for an established IKE SA with an installed CHILD SA. None came up, so it put the set-aside pair back from .previous-&lt;id>/, loaded it and re-authenticated. For ipsec-ca with --no-overlap, a partner that no longer trusts our old CA cannot carry the link on the old pair either.

## Why it happens

- the partner does not trust the CA that issued the new leaf
- the partner's edge expects another identity, or is not reachable

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
read swanctl --list-sas and the charon log on both edges, then stage the new pair again and run the rotation again
```
