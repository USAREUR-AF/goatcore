# GB-046: the local declaration is not the source of record's

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-046` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-046` or `goatbuilder explain GB-046`.

## What it means

a deploy applies only what the source of record declares (contract C3). A local copy behind it is fast-forwarded first, or refused under --frozen; a local copy ahead of it or diverged from it is always refused, because a declaration that is not in the source of record is never applied.

## Why it happens

- the checkout or the fixture's declared/ was not fast-forwarded and --frozen was passed
- a declaration was edited locally instead of in the source of record

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
fast-forward, then re-run: cp source/pins.yaml declared/pins.yaml (on a repository: git pull --ff-only)
make the change in the source of record and re-run
```
