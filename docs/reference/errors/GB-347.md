# GB-347: the copy is not well under the node's own slot cap

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-347` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-347` or `goatbuilder explain GB-347`.

## What it means

The re-seed's copy is written as WAL on the node, and that WAL counts against the slot the node keeps for the founder. The replicated tables to copy hold more than the allowed share (`--size-fraction`, 0.25 by default) of the node's `max_slot_wal_keep_size`. A copy near the cap invalidates the founder's slot on the node, and the node's next writes could not reach the founder. Nothing was changed.

## Why it happens

- the replicated tables are larger than the node's cap allows for
- the node runs with a small max_slot_wal_keep_size

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
raise max_slot_wal_keep_size on the node for the copy, then run the re-seed again
read the sizes on the founder: SELECT pg_total_relation_size(...) for the tables of the subscription's replication sets
```
