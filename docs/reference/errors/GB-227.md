# GB-227: the core still admits the partner through its own configuration

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-227` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-227` or `goatbuilder explain GB-227`.

## What it means

The core router was configured by roles/goat-zenoh from zenoh_sovereign_peerings, which declares the partner's subject and grants as sovereign-&lt;partner> ids. The back-out takes the partner's entry out of group_vars/&lt;core router group>/peerings.yml, the file admission writes it into, and runs the role's play, which restarts the router without the partner's store and then deletes that store whole (ADR 1234). After the back-out the core still declares the partner: the entry is in a file admission does not write, the back-out could not rewrite peerings.yml (GB-237), the core was admitted through the deployment's own route (FED_CORE_\*), or the role's play failed. The partner has no edge left to reach the core through.

## Why it happens

- the core admits the peering from a zenoh_sovereign_peerings entry the back-out did not write, or could not take out
- the role's play failed after the entry was taken out, and the entry was put back

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
remove the partner's entry from zenoh_sovereign_peerings by review where it is declared and run the core play (a router restart); the role then reports sovereign_from_<partner> in .undeclared-stores
after a failed play: read its output, then goatbuilder federate backout <partner> again
```
