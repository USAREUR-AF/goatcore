# GB-238: a federation verb's site flags are given where they do not belong

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-238` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-238` or `goatbuilder explain GB-238`.

## What it means

goatbuilder federate init|lint|compose|diff|verify|apply read this party's context from --context-dir, --cert, --ca-roots, --context and --profile-dir. Those flags belong to the six verbs and come after the verb: given before it they are read as the name of a partner, and given to a peering run or to `federate plan` they would be ignored.

## Why it happens

- the flags were typed after `federate` and before the verb
- the flags were given to `goatbuilder federate <partner>` or `federate plan`, which read the deployment's own declarations

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder federate lint --context-dir <dir> <package> (the verb first, then its flags)
drop the flags from a peering run or a plan
```
