# GB-160: two peerings on one forwarder would share a value

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-160` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-160` or `goatbuilder explain GB-160`.

## What it means

The peerings one forwarder relays must share no carrier interface, public port, carrier port or carrier address on it: the forwarder sends each packet by its port alone. Allocation keeps them apart across every environment the forwarder relays (by its endpoint); this refusal is the backstop for values that collide anyway.

## Why it happens

- a hand edit of peering-values.yaml gives two relayed peerings one forwarder value
- a pin under values: (public_port, carrier_port, carrier_interface, carrier_subnet) repeats another peering's value

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
pin different public_port, carrier_port, carrier_interface and carrier_subnet values under one peering's values:
or give each environment its own forwarder (forwarder: group:)
```
