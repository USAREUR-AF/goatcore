# GB-045: the published declarations are out of order or altered

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-045` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-045` or `goatbuilder explain GB-045`.

## What it means

a declaration is applied only from an intact, ordered log (contract C2). A row whose signature does not match its content, or a serial not greater than one already accepted, is refused before anything is published, so a replayed or edited declaration never reaches a machine. A new serial at an earlier digest is accepted; that is a revert.

## Why it happens

- a row of declared/publications.jsonl was edited by hand after it was published
- an older copy of the log was restored over a newer one, or a row was replayed

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
restore declared/publications.jsonl from the source of record
to go back to an earlier ref, declare it anew: goatbuilder --mock <fixture> rollback <component> 1 --env <env> --ack "<your name>"
```
