# GB-200: the federation plan's inputs changed

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-200` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-200` or `goatbuilder explain GB-200`.

## What it means

an input the saved plan rests on (versions, the agreement, a rendered file, a certificate, a resource or the acceptance policy) differs from what the plan recorded, so the approval given to that plan does not cover this change.

## Why it happens

- a phase rendered, minted or composed again after the plan was made
- the checkout, the goat binary or a declared value changed after the plan was made

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder federate plan <partner>, then review and approve the new plan
```
