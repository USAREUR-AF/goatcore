## What it means

rollback N goes back N deploys in a host's ledger, which keeps the last seven. N outside 1 to 7 names no deploy the ledger holds.

## Why it happens

- N is 0, or more than 7

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
goatbuilder rollback <component> --env <goatnet> <N from 1 to 7>
```
