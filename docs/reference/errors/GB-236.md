# GB-236: our core router's goat-zenoh role declarations do not resolve for admission

Generated from `ops/errors/catalog.yaml` by `ops/ci/check-error-catalog.py --write`; do not edit here. `GB-236` is printed by `goatbuilder`. The same text offline: `goat explain GB-236` or `goatbuilder explain GB-236`.

## What it means

Phase 10's admission names no route of its own (FED_CORE_PLAY and FED_CORE_RESTART are unset), and an inventory group of the site inventory (ops/ansible/inventory/\*.ini, not peering-\*.ini) has group_vars that configure the goat-zenoh role, so admission restarts the core through the role's own play on that group. It could not resolve that play: several groups configure the role and the peering core's address does not pick one, nothing gives the group's site_id, or no single fleet pin whose image variable the group sets names a deploy play that exists. The message names what was looked for and where. Nothing on the core changed.

## Why it happens

- two inventory groups both configure the goat-zenoh role
- the core router group's group_vars declare no site_id and the group is not named &lt;site>_hosts
- no component in ops/pins/fleet-pins.yaml has the group's image variable as its var with a deploy play

## What to do

One line per step or alternative:

```text
declare site_id in group_vars/<core router group>/
keep one inventory group per core router, or put the peering's core in the peering inventory with the same address
set FED_CORE_PLAY or FED_CORE_RESTART to name the route by hand
```
